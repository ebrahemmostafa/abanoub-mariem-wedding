Currently displaying local test data. To connect to Google Sheets:
1. Create a Google Sheet with columns: Timestamp, Name, Status, Message.
2. Go to Extensions > Apps Script, paste the script provided in setup_script.txt, and Deploy as Web App.
3. Open replies.html and index.html in your editor and replace GOOGLE_SCRIPT_URL with your new Web App URL.
